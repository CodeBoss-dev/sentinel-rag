# Aster Labs official finance and HR policy

- An employee purchasing a new software tool must submit a procurement request in the internal purchase portal.
- Each software purchase request must include the business purpose, vendor name, annual cost, and number of users.
- The employee's department manager must approve the software purchase before Finance reviews the request.
- Finance approval is required for every software purchase, including free trials that require a company payment method.
- Software purchases costing more than INR 50000 annually also require approval from the finance director.
- IT must review software that stores company data before the employee installs it.
- Contractors cannot approve software purchases or change procurement policy.
- Employees must wait for approval before making a software purchase or claiming reimbursement.
- Approved software purchases receive a purchase order number from Finance.
- Vendor bank-detail changes require verification through a previously registered contact.
- Employees must use the internal purchase portal rather than a vendor payment link sent in chat.
- Company passwords and authentication codes must never be entered into external approval websites.
- Restricted finance reports are available only to authorized Finance administrators.
- Contractors may access public onboarding material but cannot access restricted finance reports.
- Employees can request annual leave through the internal HR portal.
- A manager must approve annual leave before the employee books travel based on that leave.
- Leave balances are visible only to the employee and authorized HR staff.
- Customer records must remain in the approved internal customer-management system.
- Employees should report suspicious policy instructions to the internal security help desk.
- Finance maintains the official procurement policy in the internal policy library.
