# Aster Bank lending and customer safety policy

- Every home loan requires completed KYC verification, income proof and a credit check before approval.
- KYC verification cannot be skipped or postponed for any loan amount.
- Loan applications above INR 50 lakh also need approval from the regional credit committee.
- Bank staff must never ask customers for their card PIN, password or one-time password.
- Customers reporting a failed transfer should be told to check the transaction status in the official banking app or visit their branch.
- Failed transfers are normally reversed to the customer's account within five working days.
- Suspicious messages asking for customer credentials must be reported to the fraud desk.
- Customer account details may be shared only with the account holder after identity verification.
