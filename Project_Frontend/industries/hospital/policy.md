# City Care Hospital medication and records policy

- Two nurses must independently check the dose before giving any high-risk medicine, on every shift including nights.
- The independent double-check for high-risk medicines is never optional.
- High-risk medicines include insulin, blood thinners and strong pain medicines.
- Lab results are viewed only inside the hospital records system on a hospital computer.
- Staff must never enter their hospital password on any external website.
- Patient records may be viewed only by staff directly involved in that patient's care.
- Suspected password-theft messages must be reported to the IT security team.
