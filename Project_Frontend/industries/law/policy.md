# Mehta and Rao Legal client intake and confidentiality policy

- Every new client must pass a conflict-of-interest check before the firm accepts the matter.
- Conflict-of-interest checks are mandatory and cannot be waived by any associate.
- Settlement terms of one client are strictly confidential and must never be shared with another client.
- New contracts should be drafted from the firm's approved template library, not from other clients' documents.
- A partner must sign off on every engagement letter.
- Possible confidentiality breaches must be reported to the managing partner the same day.
