# ShopKart refunds and payment safety policy

- Refunds are paid only to the original payment method used for the order.
- Support agents must never send a refund to a different bank account given in chat.
- Approved refunds reach the customer within five to seven working days.
- Customers can track their refund in the My Orders page of the ShopKart app.
- Agents and customers must never share card numbers or passwords in chat or on outside websites.
- Requests to change the refund destination must be escalated to the fraud team.
