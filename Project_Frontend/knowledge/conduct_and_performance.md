# Aster Labs code of conduct and performance guide

- Every employee must complete the code-of-conduct acknowledgement each year in April.
- Gifts from vendors worth more than INR 2000 must be declined or reported to the compliance team.
- Employees must declare any conflict of interest, such as a relative working for a vendor, to the compliance team.
- Harassment complaints can be raised with the internal committee, and every complaint is kept confidential.
- Concerns about misconduct can be reported anonymously through the ethics hotline.
- Employees must not speak to the press about Aster Labs without approval from the communications team.
- Outside paid work needs written approval from HR and must not overlap with working hours.
- Performance reviews are held twice a year, in April and October.
- Employees set their goals with their manager in the first two weeks of April.
- Performance ratings use a five-point scale from needs improvement to outstanding.
- Promotions are announced once a year, in April.
- Each employee has a training budget of INR 40000 per year for courses and certifications.
- A training course costing more than INR 15000 needs the department head's approval.
- Employees who receive company-funded training above INR 40000 agree to stay for one year afterwards.
- Managers hold a one-to-one meeting with each team member at least once every two weeks.
- Internal job openings are posted on the HR portal for 10 days before they are advertised outside.
- An employee can apply for an internal transfer after 18 months in their current role.
- The dress code is smart casual, and formal wear is expected for client meetings.
