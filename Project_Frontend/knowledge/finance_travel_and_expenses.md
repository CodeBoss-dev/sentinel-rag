# Aster Labs travel and expense policy

- Business travel must be approved by the department manager before any booking is made.
- Flights and hotels for business travel are booked through the company travel desk.
- Domestic flights are booked in economy class.
- International flights longer than eight hours may be booked in premium economy.
- The hotel limit for business travel is INR 6000 per night in metro cities and INR 4000 per night elsewhere.
- The daily meal allowance during domestic business travel is INR 1200.
- The daily allowance for international business travel is USD 60.
- Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
- Every expense claim above INR 500 needs an original receipt or invoice.
- Approved expense claims are reimbursed with the next salary payment.
- Taxi fares to and from the airport are reimbursed for business travel.
- Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed on any expense claim.
- Client entertainment above INR 5000 needs the department head's approval in advance.
- Travel insurance is arranged by the travel desk for every international trip.
- A travel advance of up to INR 20000 can be requested five working days before an international trip.
- Unspent travel advance must be returned within seven days of returning from the trip.
- Company credit cards are issued only to employees who travel more than six times a year.
- Expense claims are submitted on the finance portal under the My Claims page.
