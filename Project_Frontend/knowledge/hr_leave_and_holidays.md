# Aster Labs leave and holiday policy

- Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Annual leave is credited at two days per month on the first working day of each month.
- Employees may carry forward up to 10 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year.
- Annual leave days above the carry-forward limit lapse on 31 December.
- Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year, and sick leave cannot be carried forward.
- Sick leave longer than two consecutive days requires a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal.
- Aster Labs observes 12 public holidays each year, and the holiday calendar is published on the HR portal every December.
- Employees may choose two optional floating holidays per year in addition to the public holidays.
- Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for eligible employees.
- Paternity leave is two weeks of paid leave, to be taken within six months of the child's birth.
- Bereavement leave is five paid days for the loss of an immediate family member.
- Annual leave of more than five consecutive days should be requested at least two weeks in advance.
- Annual leave requests are submitted through the internal HR portal and approved by the reporting manager.
- Leave taken without approval is treated as unpaid leave.
- Employees who work on a public holiday receive one compensatory day off, to be used within 60 days.
- Unused annual leave of up to 15 days is paid out when an employee leaves the company.
- Probationary employees can take annual leave only after completing three months of service.
- Half-day leave can be requested for either the first half or the second half of the working day.
- Employees can view their own leave balance on the HR portal under the My Leave page.
- Questions about leave policy should be sent to the HR help desk at extension 4100.
