# Aster Labs payroll and benefits handbook

- Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month by bank transfer.
- Payslips are available on the HR portal on the day salary is paid.
- Changes to an employee's salary bank account must be made in person at the HR desk with a cancelled cheque.
- Income tax declarations must be submitted on the HR portal by 15 April each year.
- Proof of tax-saving investments must be uploaded by 15 January.
- Provident fund contributions are 12 percent of basic salary from both the employee and the company.
- Every employee is covered by group health insurance of INR 5 lakh for themselves, their spouse and two children.
- Parents can be added to the health insurance plan at the employee's own cost during the April enrolment window.
- The annual health check-up is free for employees above 35 years of age.
- Aster Labs provides a meal allowance of INR 2200 per month through a meal card.
- The annual performance bonus is paid with the April salary.
- Employees become eligible for gratuity after five years of continuous service.
- The employee referral bonus is INR 50000, paid after the referred person completes six months.
- Salary advances of up to one month's basic pay can be requested once a year through the HR portal.
- The notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees and 15 days during probation.
- The probation period for new employees is six months.
- Full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of an employee's last working day.
- Aster Labs reimburses up to INR 3000 per quarter for gym or fitness memberships.
- Questions about payslips should be raised as a payroll ticket on the HR portal.
