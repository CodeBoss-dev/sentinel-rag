# Aster Labs working hours and remote work policy

- Standard working hours at Aster Labs are 9:30 am to 6:30 pm, Monday to Friday.
- Core hours, when every employee should be reachable, are 11:00 am to 4:00 pm.
- Employees may start their working day any time between 8:00 am and 11:00 am with their manager's agreement.
- The lunch break is one hour and is not counted as working time.
- Aster Labs follows a hybrid model with three office days and two remote days per week.
- Tuesday and Thursday are fixed office days for all teams.
- Working remotely for more than two days in a week requires the reporting manager's approval.
- Employees working remotely must use the company VPN for all internal systems.
- Remote work from outside India requires approval from HR and the security team at least 10 working days in advance.
- Overtime must be approved by the reporting manager before the extra hours are worked.
- Approved overtime is compensated with time off in lieu rather than extra pay.
- Attendance is recorded automatically when an employee badges in at the office or signs in to the VPN.
- Employees who expect to be more than 30 minutes late should inform their manager in the team channel.
- The office is open from 7:30 am to 9:30 pm on working days.
- Working in the office on a weekend requires a facilities request submitted by Friday noon.
- Employees receive a one-time home-office allowance of INR 15000 for a desk and chair after confirmation.
- The monthly internet reimbursement for hybrid employees is up to INR 1000 against a bill.
- Team meetings should be scheduled within core hours wherever possible.
