# Aster Labs accounts and password standard

- Company passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Company passwords expire every 180 days.
- Passwords are reset only through the self-service page on the internal identity portal.
- The IT help desk never asks an employee for their password.
- Two-step verification is mandatory for email, the VPN and the HR portal.
- The authenticator app is the approved method for two-step verification codes.
- An account is locked for 15 minutes after five wrong password attempts.
- Employees must not share their account with a colleague, even temporarily.
- Access to a new internal system is requested on the service desk portal and approved by the system owner.
- Access rights are reviewed by managers every six months.
- A contractor account expires automatically on the contract end date.
- Employees should store passwords only in the company-approved password manager.
- Administrator access to servers is granted for a fixed period and is logged.
- A departing employee's accounts are disabled at 6:00 pm on their last working day.
- Suspicious sign-in alerts should be reported to the security help desk immediately.
- Password hints and security questions are not used at Aster Labs.
- The same password must not be reused across company and personal accounts.
