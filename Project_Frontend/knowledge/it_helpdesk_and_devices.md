# Aster Labs IT help desk and device guide

- The IT help desk is open from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm on working days.
- IT support tickets are raised on the internal service desk portal or by calling extension 5000.
- A new employee receives a company laptop on their first day from the IT desk on the second floor.
- Company laptops are replaced every four years.
- A request for a new or replacement laptop needs the department manager's approval on the service desk portal.
- A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT help desk within two hours.
- Employees must not install software that is not on the approved software list.
- The approved software list is published on the IT wiki and updated every month.
- Company laptops install security updates automatically every Wednesday evening.
- Employees should restart their laptop at least once a week so that updates can finish.
- The office guest wifi is for visitors only and must not be used for company work.
- Printers are available on every floor and require badge sign-in to release a print job.
- Employees may connect one personal phone to company email after enrolling it in device management.
- A second monitor, keyboard and mouse can be requested through the service desk portal without manager approval.
- Meeting-room video equipment problems are reported by scanning the code on the room panel.
- Shared drives are backed up every night and files can be restored for up to 90 days.
- Company laptops must be returned to the IT desk on the employee's last working day.
- High-priority IT tickets, such as being unable to log in, are answered within one hour.
- Standard IT tickets are answered within one working day.
