# Aster Labs office and facilities guide

- The Aster Labs office is on floors two to five of the Meridian Tech Park building.
- The cafeteria is on the fifth floor and serves lunch from 12:30 pm to 2:30 pm.
- Tea and coffee are free at the pantry on every floor.
- Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar, for a maximum of two hours per booking.
- The Kaveri training room on the third floor seats 40 people.
- Car parking is in basement two, and parking stickers are issued by the facilities desk.
- Two-wheeler parking is free in basement one.
- The company shuttle runs from the metro station to the office every 20 minutes between 8:30 am and 10:30 am.
- The evening shuttle to the metro station leaves at 6:45 pm and 7:45 pm.
- A lost access card is replaced by the facilities desk for a fee of INR 300.
- Visitors must be registered on the visitor portal one day before they arrive.
- The wellness room on the fourth floor can be used for rest or prayer.
- The first-aid kit and a trained first-aider are available at every floor's reception.
- Fire drills are held twice a year, and the assembly point is the north car park.
- Couriers and personal parcels are collected from the mail room on the second floor.
- The office gym on the fifth floor is open from 6:30 am to 9:00 pm.
- Air-conditioning after 8:00 pm must be requested from the facilities desk by 5:00 pm.
- Lost property is kept at the ground-floor reception for 30 days.
