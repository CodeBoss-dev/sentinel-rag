# Aster Labs new joiner guide

- New joiners report to the reception on the ground floor at 9:30 am on their first day.
- New joiners collect their access card from the facilities desk after the first-day photograph.
- The first-day orientation session runs from 10:30 am to 1:00 pm in the Kaveri training room.
- Every new joiner is assigned a buddy from their own team for the first month.
- New joiners must upload identity and education documents to the HR portal within their first week.
- Mandatory security and code-of-conduct training must be completed within 14 days of joining.
- New joiners meet their reporting manager for goal setting in their second week.
- The probation review meeting takes place at the end of the fifth month.
- New joiners get access to email and the team chat on their first day.
- Access to project systems is requested by the new joiner's manager.
- The new joiner welcome kit includes a laptop bag, a notebook and a water bottle.
- New joiners are added to the all-hands channel, where company announcements are posted.
- The company all-hands meeting is held on the first Friday of every month at 4:00 pm.
- New joiners can find the organisation chart on the HR portal home page.
- Health insurance cards are issued within three weeks of joining.
- New joiners should set up two-step verification on their first day with help from the IT desk.
