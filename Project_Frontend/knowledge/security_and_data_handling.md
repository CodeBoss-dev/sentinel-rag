# Aster Labs information security and data handling policy

- Aster Labs classifies information as public, internal, confidential or restricted.
- Confidential information may be shared only with employees who need it for their work.
- Restricted information may be opened only by people named on the document's access list.
- Customer personal data must not be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.
- Confidential files sent outside the company must be shared through the secure file-transfer portal.
- Employees must lock their screen whenever they leave their desk.
- Visitors must be accompanied by an employee at all times inside the office.
- Printed confidential documents must be shredded in the bins marked for secure disposal.
- USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops by default.
- A suspected security incident must be reported to the security help desk at extension 5900 within one hour.
- Phishing emails should be reported using the Report Phishing button in the mail client.
- Employees complete security awareness training every year in January.
- Company data must not be entered into public AI chat tools.
- Customer data is retained for seven years and then deleted.
- Laptop disks are encrypted and this encryption must not be turned off.
- Access cards must not be lent to other people.
- Photographs are not allowed in the server room or the finance area.
- Security policy exceptions need written approval from the head of security.
- The security team runs a simulated phishing exercise every quarter.
