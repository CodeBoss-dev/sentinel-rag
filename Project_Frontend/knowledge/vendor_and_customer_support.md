# Aster Labs vendor and customer support handbook

- A new vendor is onboarded only after Finance verifies its registration and tax documents.
- Vendor contracts above INR 10 lakh per year need legal review before signing.
- Vendor invoices are paid within 45 days of an approved invoice.
- Vendor invoices must be sent to the accounts payable mailbox with the purchase order number.
- Customer support hours are 9:00 am to 9:00 pm, seven days a week.
- A customer complaint must receive a first reply within four working hours.
- Refunds up to INR 10000 can be approved by a support team lead.
- Refunds above INR 10000 need approval from the support manager and Finance.
- Customer identity must be verified with two details from their account before any account change.
- Support agents must never ask a customer for their password or one-time code.
- Customer complaints that mention legal action are escalated to the legal team the same day.
- Service outages are announced on the status page within 15 minutes of being confirmed.
- Customer feedback surveys are sent after every closed support ticket.
- Vendor performance is reviewed every quarter by the department that owns the contract.
- Non-disclosure agreements must be signed before any confidential document is shared with a vendor.
- A vendor's access to company systems ends on the last day of the contract.
